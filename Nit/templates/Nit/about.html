About GIS Survey

Powerful land record management for modern urban planning

What We Do

GIS Survey Management System is a comprehensive platform built for urban planners, surveyors, and administrators to handle residential, commercial, and industrial land records with precision and ease.

Our system provides real-time mapping, property boundary visualization, and intelligent report generation. It supports ward-level data management, corporate zoning, and integrates seamlessly with external GIS layers.

Key Features

  • Residential Surveys Property lines, building footprints, ownership data
  • Commercial Parcels Business licenses, floor area, parking capacity
  • Industrial Zoning Usage permits, infrastructure, environmental zones
  • GIS Dashboard Interactive maps, heatmaps, demographic overlays
  • Reports & Export PDF, CSV, GeoJSON with custom templates
